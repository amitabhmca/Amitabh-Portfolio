Meet Amitabh!

👋 Hi, I’m Amitabh Pandey

My professional path has followed the evolution of enterprise computing: from Linux servers, network engineering and operational resilience to virtualization, cloud systems, high-performance computing and, increasingly, AI infrastructure.

I architect AI factories across both hardware and software: compute, networking and storage on one side, and orchestration, scheduling and observability on the other. I also build the operating processes that keep services running. The common thread is a systems perspective, designing and operating the foundations that applications and AI workloads depend on.
